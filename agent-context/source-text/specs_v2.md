31 | Meet Dr. Hyo-won Kang
33 | Born in Korea, he majored in theology at Sungwha Theology, completed his Master's degree in Oriental Medicine at Samra Acupuncture School, and pursued his Doctor of Acupuncture and Oriental Medicine (DAOM) at South Baylor Acupuncture School. Coming from a family of traditional practitioners, he learned acupuncture and herbal medicine from a young age. He also studied therapeutic massage, osteopathy, and spiritual healing under various mentors. Gifted with sharp intuition and refined tactile senses, he possesses a strong ability to diagnose patients through pulse diagnosis and physical palpation, identifying the root causes of illnesses and determining the appropriate treatments. Recognizing that he is human and cannot accurately diagnose or cure every condition, he always pours his utmost sincerity and dedication into patient diagnosis and treatment. Before diagnosing and treating his patients, he offers a prayer, wishing for God's miracles to be with everyone who seeks his care.

As a third-generation descendant carrying on a traditional medicine heritage, he possesses 28 years of rich clinical experience and the ability to interpret traditional medicine through a modern lens. He has the ability to diagnose unseen internal illnesses within the body. Rather than merely treating surface-level symptoms, his treatments focus on identifying the root cause of an illness to provide fundamental healing at its source. Every individual has a different constitution, family diet, lifestyle habits, genetic factors, and life circumstances. Moreover, different illnesses manifest depending on the seasons, weather, and temperature. By analyzing these diverse factors, treatments must be tailored to the individual. His 28 years of experience and diverse studies enable him to provide you with personalized care. Choosing Dr. Kang means encountering his experience and capability, which have been dedicated to disease treatment for a lifetime. He is truly sincere about healing his patients.

100 | #7. Post-Treatment Wellness Guide
101 | Healing doesn’t stop when you leave Marin Holy Hill Acupuncture Clinic. To ensure your body processes the treatment effectively and to maximize your recovery, please follow these simple aftercare recommendations.
102 | Quick Guide: Dos & Don’ts
103 | Please Avoid
104 | High-intensity workouts
105 | Ice-cold drinks or raw meals
106 | Alcohol and stimulants
107 | Overeating or heavy, greasy food
108 | Digital strain (excessive screen time)
110 | Please Focus On
111 | Staying hydrated with filtered water
112 | Warm, easy-to-digest nourishment
113 | Listening to your body’s signals
114 | Deep rest and quality sleep
115 | Keeping the treated areas warm
117 | Specific Aftercare by Treatment
118 | Acupuncture Recovery
119 | It is very common to feel deeply relaxed, light-headed, or even slightly fatigued after a session.
120 | Allow yourself time to rest if you feel sleepy.
121 | Avoid touching or scratching the points where needles were placed.
122 | Minor bruising is rare but normal; it will fade within a few days.
123 | Cupping Therapy Care
124 | The characteristic circular marks and slight tenderness are signs of toxin release and increased blood flow. They typically disappear in 3–5 days.
125 | Crucial: Keep the area covered and protected from cold drafts. Wait at least 24 hours before taking a cold shower.
126 | Refrain from scratching the marks as the skin may be sensitive.
127 | Moxibustion (Heat Therapy)
128 | You may feel a lingering, pleasant warmth in the treated area for several hours.
129 | Protect your body from sudden temperature changes (cold wind or cold water).
130 | Sip warm tea or water to help maintain the improved circulation.
131 | Medical Massage, Foot, and Manual therapy
132 | You might experience mild "good" soreness similar to the feeling after a workout.
133 | Increase your water intake to help flush out any metabolic waste released during therapy.
134 | Avoid heavy lifting or strenuous physical labor for the remainder of the day.
135 | Herbal Medicine Protocol
136 | Consistency is key—take your herbal formulas exactly as prescribed by your practitioner.
137 | Try to avoid spicy or highly processed foods, as they can interfere with the herbs' effectiveness.
138 | If you experience any unexpected changes, please notify us immediately.
